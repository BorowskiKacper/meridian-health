# Northwind Health Claims and Appeals Guide

Fictional insurer for a hackathon demo. Northwind Health is not a real company and this is not a real coverage policy.

## Claim submission

In-network providers submit claims electronically within 180 days of the date of service. Claims submitted after 180 days are denied for timely filing, and the member may not be billed for them. Out-of-network claims on the PPO plan may be submitted by the provider or the member within 365 days.

Clean claims are paid within 30 days of receipt. A claim missing required information is pended, and the provider is notified within 15 days of what is missing.

## Common denial reasons

No prior authorization on file for a service that requires it. Service not covered under the member's plan. Coverage was not active on the date of service. Duplicate claim. Diagnosis code does not support medical necessity for the billed procedure. Screening service billed with a diagnostic code, which can be corrected and resubmitted.

## Corrected claims

A claim denied for a coding error is corrected and resubmitted, not appealed. Corrected claims must be submitted within 90 days of the original denial and must reference the original claim number.

## Appeals

A member or a provider acting for the member may file a first-level appeal within 180 days of the denial. The appeal should include the denial letter, the reason the decision is wrong, and supporting chart notes or letters of medical necessity. Northwind decides standard pre-service appeals within 30 days and post-service appeals within 60 days. Expedited appeals for urgent care are decided within 72 hours.

If the first-level appeal is upheld, the member may request a second-level internal review within 60 days, and then an independent external review through the state within 4 months of the final internal decision.

## Member cost sharing

PPO plan: $30 primary care copay, $60 specialist copay, $1,500 individual deductible, 20% coinsurance in network after the deductible, $5,000 individual out-of-pocket maximum. HMO plan: $20 primary care copay, $45 specialist copay with a referral, no deductible for office visits, $4,000 individual out-of-pocket maximum. Preventive services are covered at no cost share in network on both plans.
