# Northwind Health Prior Authorization Requirements

Fictional insurer for a hackathon demo. Northwind Health is not a real company and this is not a real coverage policy.

## How prior authorization works

The ordering provider submits the request through the Northwind provider portal or by fax with the order, the diagnosis codes, and the supporting chart notes. Standard requests are decided within 7 calendar days. Urgent requests, where waiting could seriously harm the member's health, are decided within 72 hours. An approval is valid for 90 days from the decision date unless the letter says otherwise.

A service that needs prior authorization and is performed without it is denied. The member is not billed for an in-network service denied only because the provider did not request authorization.

## Services that require prior authorization

Advanced imaging: MRI, CT, PET, and nuclear cardiology studies in an outpatient setting. CT colonography for screening and low-dose CT for lung cancer screening are exempt.

Sleep studies, both in-lab and home sleep apnea testing.

Genetic testing, including BRCA1 and BRCA2 testing and hereditary cancer panels. Requests must show the member meets family history criteria.

Specialty drugs and biologics, including PCSK9 inhibitors, GLP-1 receptor agonists prescribed for weight management, and biologics for rheumatoid arthritis, psoriasis, and inflammatory bowel disease.

Elective inpatient admissions, inpatient rehabilitation, skilled nursing facility stays, and home health care.

Durable medical equipment with a purchase price over $500, including CPAP machines after the first 90 days.

## Services that do not require prior authorization

Office visits with in-network primary care providers and specialists, routine laboratory tests including lipid panels, hemoglobin A1c, and comprehensive metabolic panels, screening colonoscopy, screening mammography, vaccines, and emergency care.

## Step therapy

PCSK9 inhibitors are approved only after the member has taken a high-intensity statin, or the maximum tolerated statin dose, together with ezetimibe for at least 3 months and LDL cholesterol remains at or above 70 mg/dL in a member with established cardiovascular disease, or at or above 100 mg/dL otherwise. Documented statin intolerance to two different statins satisfies the statin requirement.

GLP-1 receptor agonists for type 2 diabetes are approved after a trial of metformin of at least 3 months, unless metformin is contraindicated or the member has established cardiovascular disease or chronic kidney disease.

## Peer-to-peer review

If a request is denied for medical necessity, the ordering provider may request a peer-to-peer conversation with a Northwind medical director within 14 days of the denial. A peer-to-peer review does not replace the member's right to appeal.
