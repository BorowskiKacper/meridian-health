# Northwind Health Preventive Services Policy

Fictional insurer for a hackathon demo. Northwind Health is not a real company and this is not a real coverage policy.

## Scope

This policy applies to all Northwind Health PPO and HMO members. It describes which preventive services are covered with no cost sharing when performed by an in-network provider. Out-of-network preventive services on the PPO plan are subject to the deductible and 40% coinsurance. The HMO plan does not cover out-of-network preventive services except in an emergency.

## Covered at no cost share in network

Northwind Health covers, at no copay, coinsurance, or deductible, preventive services that have an A or B rating from the United States Preventive Services Task Force on the first day of the plan year. This includes blood pressure screening, lipid screening, prediabetes and type 2 diabetes screening for adults aged 35 to 70 with overweight or obesity, cervical cancer screening, screening mammography, low-dose CT lung cancer screening for eligible adults, osteoporosis screening, hepatitis C screening, depression screening, and colorectal cancer screening.

The annual preventive visit is covered once per plan year at no cost share. Problems addressed during the preventive visit may be billed separately and are subject to the member's office visit copay.

## Colorectal cancer screening

Average-risk members are covered for colorectal cancer screening starting at age 45. Covered screening tests are a fecal immunochemical test (FIT) every year, a multitarget stool DNA test every 1 to 3 years, CT colonography every 5 years, flexible sigmoidoscopy every 5 years, or colonoscopy every 10 years.

Members at increased risk are covered for screening colonoscopy at an earlier age and a shorter interval when the ordering clinician documents the risk factor. A first-degree relative diagnosed with colorectal cancer or an advanced adenoma qualifies the member for screening colonoscopy starting at age 40, or 10 years before the relative's age at diagnosis, whichever is earlier, repeated every 5 years. The order must list the family history diagnosis code.

A follow-up colonoscopy after a positive stool-based screening test is part of the screening process and is covered at no cost share. Removal of polyps during a screening colonoscopy does not change the visit to diagnostic for cost-sharing purposes. Anesthesia and pathology for a covered screening colonoscopy are covered at no cost share.

Screening colonoscopy does not require prior authorization. A colonoscopy ordered for symptoms such as bleeding, anemia, or a change in bowel habits is diagnostic and is subject to the member's specialist copay and deductible.

## Breast cancer screening

Screening mammography, including digital breast tomosynthesis, is covered every year for members aged 40 to 74 at no cost share. Supplemental breast MRI for members with dense breasts or a lifetime risk above 20% requires prior authorization.

## Statins for prevention

Generic moderate-intensity statins are covered at no cost share for members aged 40 to 75 who have one or more cardiovascular risk factors and a 10-year cardiovascular risk of 10% or greater. Brand-name statins are covered at the tier 3 copay.
